## English (`en`)

**Title:** Snoon: Alarm Clock & Timer

**Short description:** Reliable alarms, smart groups, interval schedules, timer and world clock.

**Full description:**

Snoon is an Android clock designed for schedules that are more complicated than a single alarm. Create one-time or weekly alarms, generate a complete series inside a time range, and organize recurring alarms into groups.

Pause a group until a selected date when you take a holiday. Add exception dates when alarms must stay silent, or shift today's alarms without changing the permanent schedule.

Key features include actionable lock-screen alarms, configurable snooze, ringtone and vibration settings, increasing volume, automatic silence, optional math or shake-to-dismiss tasks, a morning routine, alarm history and a Reliability Center for Android permissions and battery restrictions.

Snoon also includes a world clock, stopwatch, custom timer, sleep schedule and local JSON backup. Your data remains on your device. There are no accounts, ads or tracking SDKs.
