Snoon Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Snoon stores alarms, groups, sleep schedules, world clocks, settings, selected language and alarm history only on the user's device. It has no account system, ads, analytics or tracking SDK and does not send personal data to a server.

Permissions

  • Notifications: upcoming, snoozed and ringing alarm controls.
  • Exact alarms: delivery at the time selected by the user.
  • Full-screen intents: alarm controls on the lock screen.
  • Vibration: only when enabled by the user.
  • Boot completed: restore alarms after a restart.
  • Foreground media playback: reliable audio while ringing.

Backups

JSON backups are created only when requested. Android's system picker controls the save location; Snoon does not upload the file.

Data deletion

Data can be deleted in Snoon or by clearing Snoon's application data in Android settings.

Contact

A developer support email address must be added before public release.
